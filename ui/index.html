Losses are included: NVIDIA leads four rows on the H100 — batch 16, 32 and 64, and time-to-first-token — and those are open engineering fronts, not omissions. Open labels are marked: a row whose correctness adjudication is still outstanding says so in its own basis line. Withdrawn results stay withdrawn — several earlier HIVE figures were retired when their measurement method proved faulty, and none of them appear here.
